🦉 Welcome to Sanctuary Studio

A bright, warm place to learn

Sanctuary Studio is a physical learning centre in Kathmandu for children from Kindergarten to Middle School — small classes, real attention, and a space that feels like a second home.

Book a visit Explore programs

Why families choose us

Small groups, flexed around your child

We keep groups small on purpose — so pace, pairing, and even schedule can bend to fit your child, instead of fitting your child into a fixed program.

A space that feels like home

Bright rooms, comfortable corners, room to play — built to feel welcoming and relaxed from day one, not clinical or rushed.

Any stage, any starting point

Kindergarten through Middle School, with no rigid grade-level boxes — we meet your child where they are and build from there.

Direct access to the people teaching your child

As a hands-on, growing team, you talk to the people actually working with your child — not a front desk relaying messages.
