How it works

  • Open to children in Kindergarten through Middle School.
  • Write in your own words — it can be a story, a poem, or a short essay on any topic.
  • A parent or guardian must submit the entry on the child's behalf, with contact details so we can reach you if your child wins.
  • Entrant's name, school, and the full entry are published on our Writing Archive once submitted — a space for every child's writing to be read, not just the winner's. Parent/guardian contact details are always kept private and only visible to Sanctuary Studio staff.
  • Winners are announced every 6 months and contacted directly.
