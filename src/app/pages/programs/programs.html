Our programs

Age-appropriate learning, from your child's first classroom to the transition into middle school.

Ages 4–5

Kindergarten

A gentle, play-based introduction to letters, numbers, and social skills. Small group sizes so every child gets attention.

  • Half-day and full-day options
  • Low student-to-teacher ratio
  • Focus on early literacy and motor skills
Ages 6–10

Lower & Upper Primary

A structured curriculum covering core subjects, with room for creativity through art, music, and group projects.

  • English, math, science, social studies
  • Weekly art and activity sessions
  • Regular progress updates for parents
Ages 11–13

Middle School

Preparing students for greater independence — deeper subject material, study skills, and early guidance on interests and strengths.

  • Subject specialist teachers
  • Study skills and time management
  • Small electives based on interest
